Auto grazing and the season planner

Plan once. The herd moves on time, every time, and you can see the plan against what really happened.

Auto grazing

When the plan says move, the tags get the new line 5 minutes before and play the new grass chime. The plan is stored on every tag.

Plan against actual

The planner fills in real grazing from the tags. No paperwork.

Stocking rate

Animal units per hectare or acre for the whole place and each mob.

Feed demand

What each mob eats per day against the feed in front of it.

Grazing days

How long each break will last before you open it.

Rest periods

Days since each paddock was last grazed, with a target you set.
