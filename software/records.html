Health, genetics and lifetime records

Tag a calf the day it is born and keep its whole story in one place, from birth weight to sale.

Tagged at birth

Record the calving and the calf gets its record that day: born, dam, sire, birth weight.

Genetics

Dam, sire, breed and genetic values from your breed association records, on every animal.

Health log

Vaccines and treatments, with the withdrawal date worked out for you.

Weights

Birth, weaning and every scale weight since, with daily gain.

Behaviour

Time spent grazing, resting and walking, and alerts when it changes.

Moves

Every paddock it has been in and when, from the tags.

Ready for the vet and the buyer

Pull up the full history in the field with a phone tap, or export it for a buyer, a vet or a breed registry.

Your records are yours. Export everything any time.
