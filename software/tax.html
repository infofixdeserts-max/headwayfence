Farm tax, filled in as you go

Every expense lands on the right line of your country's farm tax form. At year end, send your accountant one file.

United StatesSchedule F (Form 1040)
AustraliaBusiness and professional items schedule, P8, with BAS
South AfricaITR12 farming income, with VAT201
CanadaT2042 Statement of Farming Activities, 2025
United KingdomSA103F Self-employment (full), tax year 2025-26 (6 Apr 2025 to 5 Apr 2026), with SA100 Tax Return
IrelandForm 11 Income Tax Return 2025 (ROS), Panel B Self-Employed Income, Extracts From Accounts lines 124-168
New ZealandIR3F Farming income (attaches to the IR3 Individual tax return), year ended 31 March 2026
BrazilDemonstrativo da Atividade Rural (ficha da DIRPF 2026, ano-calendario 2025), pessoa fisica produtor rural
ArgentinaGanancias Personas Humanas, F.711 (ARCA), tax year 2025, third category farm income
MexicoDeclaracion Anual de Personas Fisicas 2025 (SAT online), Actividad empresarial - Titulo IV, Cap. II, Seccion I LISR (persona fisica, actividad ganadera)
KenyaIT1 Individual Income Tax Return (resident individual, farming income section), KRA iTax, year of income 2025
ZimbabweITF 12C Income Tax Self Assessment Return, tax year ended 31 December 2025
Any other countryA simple income and expense report your accountant can use.

Drafts only. Your accountant checks and files.
