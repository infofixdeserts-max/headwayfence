Questions about Headway

What is Headway?

Solar ear tags that act as a fence you draw on a map, plus one app for grazing, the herd, money and tax.

What is virtual fencing?

You draw the fence on a map. When an animal walks toward the line, its tag plays a tone and it turns back. No posts, no wire.

Is it a legal boundary fence?

No. Keep your boundary and road fences. Headway is for moving and holding stock inside your place.

Does it hurt the animals?

Sound does the work. A stimulus is only a last step, with strict limits. Animals walking back or running are left alone.

Why an ear tag and not a collar?

No neck strap to stretch, snap or catch on a fence.

How is the tag powered?

It is charged by the sun. It is sealed and built to last 15 years.

What if there is no cell signal?

The tags keep the fence and the grazing plan on their own.

Can it warn me about lightning?

Yes. The app tells you when a storm is getting close to the herd.

What does it cost?

The app has no subscription. The tags run on a cost-effective subscription. Prices come before the first sale.

Which animals does it work with?

All livestock: cattle, sheep, goats and more.

Which tag goes on which animal?

The herd tag goes on every animal. A few animals in each herd also wear a lead tag or a camera tag in the other ear.

When does an animal get its tag?

Its record starts the day it is born, linked to its mother. The Headway tag goes on at weaning, when the ear is big enough.

Can it tell if an animal is sick?

The herd tag tracks heart rate, temperature, blood oxygen and how the animal moves. When one of them drifts from that animal's normal, you get an alert, often before you could see it.

Can it watch for predators?

Yes. The tags listen for wolves, coyotes and dogs, and notice when the herd bunches up or runs. You get an alert with where it happened.

Can it tell me about my land?

Yes. Camera tags take pictures of the grass and plants as the herd grazes, and every tag listens for birds and insects. Over time you see your land getting better, or not.

How does it save me time?

No fence to build or move, moves that happen on their own, and one list of today's jobs with the most urgent first.

Does the tag weigh animals?

No. It works with a walk-over scale, so each weight saves to the right animal on its own.

Can a vet see records in the field?

Yes. Hold a phone to the tag and the record opens.

Which tax forms does it fill in?

The farm tax form for the country you are in. The United States first, then Canada, Australia and England. Your accountant checks and files.

When can I buy tags?

Tags are in testing now. Get in touch and we will keep you posted.
