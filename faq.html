Questions about Headway

What is Headway?

Headway is a virtual fencing system for cattle made by Headway Ag LLC in Stuart, Florida. Solar ear tags hold a virtual fence line, watch every animal and detect lightning. A free app plans the grazing, moves the herd, keeps the books and fills in farm tax forms.

What is virtual fencing?

A virtual fence is a line drawn on a map in an app. Each animal wears a device that knows where it is and where the line is. As the animal walks toward the line it hears a tone, and most turn around. It lets you move cattle and cross fence pastures without building fence.

Is a virtual fence a legal perimeter fence?

No. It is a management tool for moving and holding cattle inside your place. Keep the permanent fence along roads and property lines that the law and your insurer require.

Does it hurt the cattle?

Sound does almost all the work. A mild stimulus is only ever the last step, with hard limits per crossing and per day. Tags never cue an animal walking back in or running, and go quiet if an animal ends up well outside the line.

Why an ear tag and not a collar?

Ranchers already tag ears. A tag has no neck strap to stretch, snap or catch on a fence, and it suits hairy breeds without shaving.

How is the tag powered?

A small solar panel on every tag charges it each day. The tag is sealed, so there are no batteries to swap.

What if there is no cell signal?

The grazing plan is stored on every tag, so planned moves still happen with no signal. A tag to tag herd link and a yard base station are in development for dead spots, and the app has a low-data mode for phone satellite messaging where supported.

Can it detect lightning?

Yes. Each tag hears lightning strikes, estimates how far away they are and tells you when a storm is getting closer to the herd.

Is the app free?

Yes. The Headway app is in development. It will be free to use under the Headway Free Use License, including the grazing, money and tax tools. Tags are sold separately.

Which countries does the tax tool cover?

The United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, Ireland, Australia, New Zealand, South Africa, Brazil, Argentina, Mexico, Kenya and Zimbabwe, plus a plain income and expense pack for anywhere else. Your accountant checks and files.

Which animals does Headway work with?

Headway is being built for beef and dairy cattle first.

Can Headway guide, split or move the herd?

Yes. You can walk a mob down a moving virtual lane to new ground, or split calves, steers or cows due to calve into their own mob with its own line, from the app.

Can it weigh cattle automatically?

Yes, with a walk-over scale at the water point. On weigh days the herd is guided to water through the scale lane, every few days on a schedule you pick, and each weight lands on that animal's record. Each Headway tag carries a standard livestock EID chip, so scales and readers that read EID tags work.

Can a vet see an animal's record in the field?

Yes. Hold a phone to the tag and the record opens. The tag carries its ID and key health notes, so the basics show even with no signal.

Does Headway keep health and genetics records?

Yes. Each animal can be tagged at birth and keeps its whole history: birth and weaning weights, dam and sire, breed and genetic values from your breed association, vaccines, treatments with withdrawal dates, pregnancy checks, weights and moves.
