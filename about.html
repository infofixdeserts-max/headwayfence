About Headway

Headway Ag LLC is a ranch technology company in Stuart, Florida. We build Headway: solar ear tags that hold a virtual fence for cattle, and a free app that runs the rest of the ranch.

Why we build it

Fence is slow and costly to build and move. Good grazing needs the herd in the right place for the right time, every time. Headway makes that a few taps instead of a few days of work.

We build on Holistic Management: people, money and land planned together. That is why the app does grazing, money and tax, not just fence lines.

How we work

  • Sound first, with welfare limits written into the tag.
  • An ear tag, not a collar.
  • Software free for every rancher. Your data is yours.
  • Honest status: tags are in testing, pilot first.
