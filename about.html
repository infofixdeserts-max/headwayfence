About Headway

Headway Ag LLC builds Headway: solar ear tags that hold a virtual fence, and one app for the rest of the place.

Why we build it

Fence is slow and costly to build and move. Good grazing needs the herd in the right place at the right time. Headway makes that a few taps, not a few days of work.

We build on Holistic Management: people, money and land planned together.

How we work

  • Sound first, with welfare rules built into the tag.
  • An ear tag, not a collar.
  • No subscription for the app. Your data is yours.
  • Tags are in testing. We will tell you when they are ready.
