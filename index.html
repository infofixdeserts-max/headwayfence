Draw the fence on a map. Solar ear tags hold it. One app runs the grazing, the herd, the money and the tax.

Meet the Headway ear tag.

It goes in like any ear tag. It plays a tone at the line, keeps track of every animal and warns you about lightning. Charged by the sun.

What the tag does

Built for computer and tablet. Works on your phone too.

The big map and the planner are easier on a big screen. Your phone does the quick jobs in the paddock.

What Headway does

Everything a virtual fence should do, plus the rest of the place: weights, health, money and tax.

Live map

See every animal on your farm map.

Virtual fences

Draw a paddock or a strip on the map. No posts, no wire.

Move the herd

Move a mob to fresh grass from your chair.

Auto grazing

Set the plan once. The herd moves on time, even with no signal.

Keep-out zones

Keep stock off creek banks, the yard or anywhere else. As many as you need.

Guide and split

Walk a mob to new ground, or split calves, steers or springers into their own group.

Pause

Turn the fence off for a mob while you feed or work them.

Lightning alerts

Know when a storm is close to the herd, and move them in one tap.

Health monitoring

Heart rate, temperature and more on every animal, so you see trouble before it shows.

Land and wildlife

The herd's tags see and hear your land: grass, weeds, birds, insects and predators.

Save time

One list of today's jobs. Moves, weigh days and reminders run on their own.

Problem alerts

Animals that stop moving, wander off alone or leave the place show up first.

Your own alerts

Set alerts for what matters to you, like a cow going off alone to calve.

Job pins

Drop a pin on a broken gate and it goes on today's list.

Grazing maps

See what was grazed hard and how long each paddock has rested.

Grazing planner

Plan the season. The tags fill in what really happened.

Weight records

Works with a walk-over scale, so weights save to the right animal with no typing.

Health and breeding

Treatments, withdrawal dates, calving and family lines in one place.

Phone tap

Hold your phone to the tag to see that animal's record.

Money

Plan the year and track every dollar.

Farm tax

Expenses land on your farm tax form as you go.

People, money and land, planned together.

Headway is built on Holistic Management. Every plan is checked against what you want for your family, your money and your land.

  • Today screen: the most urgent job first.
  • Rest days for you and your crew.
  • Grazing that leaves the land better every year.

Give you back your time.

No fence to build or move. The herd moves on its own. You get one short list of what needs you today.

  • Today's jobs, most urgent first.
  • Reminders for vaccines, weigh days and calving.
  • Plan the week for you and your crew.
