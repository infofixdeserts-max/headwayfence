Virtual fencing for cattle, and the whole ranch in one app.

Solar ear tags hold the paddock line, watch every animal and listen for lightning. The Headway app has no subscription. It plans the grazing, moves the herd on schedule, keeps the books and fills in your farm tax forms.

Meet the Headway ear tag.

The tag sits on one side of the ear and a small pod on the other, joined by a pin through the ear. Solar on top and sealed for life outside. It plays the tone, finds the herd and hears the weather.

What the tag keeps track of

Built for PC and tablet. Works on your phone too.

The big map and the planner are made for a screen you can actually see. Your phone does the quick jobs out in the paddock.

Everything a virtual fence should do, and the rest of the ranch too.

We listened to what ranchers want from virtual fencing, kept what works, and added the parts of ranching most systems leave out: lightning, money and tax.

Live map of every animal

GPS position of each animal on a farm map, live, mob by mob.

Virtual fences and breaks

Draw a paddock or a strip break on the map, or make them from a template.

Move mobs from the app

Shift a mob to the next break from your chair. The line goes to every tag.

Auto grazing

The grazing plan is stored on every tag, so moves happen on time even with no signal.

Keep-out zones

Keep cattle out of a creek, the house yard or a pivot tower inside a paddock.

Guide, split and move

Walk a mob down a moving lane to new ground, or split calves, steers or springers into their own mob.

Pause tags

Turn cues off for a mob while you feed in the yard or work them through the pens.

Lightning detection

Each tag hears lightning and tells you how far away it is and if it is getting closer.

No movement alerts

An animal that has not moved, or stands apart from the herd, goes to the top of your list.

Theft alerts

An animal moving at truck speed sends an alert straight away.

Map pins for jobs

Drop a pin on a broken gate or a weed patch and it lands on today's job list.

Replay tracks

See where a cow has been. Handy for finding a new calf.

Heat and rest maps

See how hard each part of a paddock was grazed, and how long it has rested.

Season grazing planner

Plan which mob grazes where and when. The tags fill in what really happened.

Feed demand and stocking rate

Animal units, feed per head per day and grazing days left in each break.

Pasture observations

Notes and cover per paddock, kept as a history you can use.

Behaviour trends

Hours spent grazing, resting and walking, day by day, from how each animal moves.

Automatic weight records

The tag does not weigh. It tells a walk-over scale who is on it, so each weight is saved to the right animal on its own.

Health and genetics

Treatments, vaccines, withdrawal dates, dam, sire and breed records, all in one place.

Tagged at birth

Tag a calf the day it is born and keep its whole life history, from birth weight to sale.

Tap a tag

Hold a phone to the tag to pull up the animal's record in the field, even with no signal.

Money and cash flow

Plan the year, then see what really happens, month by month.

Farm tax forms

Filled in as you go, for 12 countries plus a plain pack for anywhere else.

People, money and land, planned together.

Headway is built on Holistic Management. The app looks at your people, your money and your land at the same time, and checks every plan against what you are trying to do.

  • Today screen: the worst problem first, nothing you do not need.
  • Rest days protected for you and your crew.
  • Grazing that leaves the ground better every year.
