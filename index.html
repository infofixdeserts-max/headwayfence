The Technology

Know every animal, every minute.

Headway replaces barbed wire with a self-powered ear-tag platform. Draw paddock boundaries from your phone, guide the herd with humane audio cues, and stream health and land data from every animal, day and night. One system for the whole operation.

Virtual Fencing

Draw paddock boundaries in a mobile app and move the herd remotely. Rotation, laneways, and fire breaks without a single post in the ground.

Self-Powered

Triple energy harvesting: solar, body heat, and the motion of the ear itself. No charging, no battery swaps, year after year.

Herd Intelligence

Temperature, location, and behavior stream from every animal over mesh, cellular, and satellite links into one live picture.

Storm Sense

Onboard sensors detect lightning strikes and brush-fire heat locally, warning the herd and the rancher before regional alerts arrive.

What It Senses

A thousand data points per animal.

Every tag watches the animal, the land, and the sky at the same time, then feeds one shared picture to your phone, tablet, or desktop.
