The Technology

A fence you draw. A herd that listens.

Since 1866, livestock management has been fenced in. Headway replaces barbed wire with a twin ear-tag platform: virtual boundaries drawn from your phone, enforced through humane audio cue and gentle guidance — while the tags watch over every animal, day and night.

Virtual Fencing

Draw paddock boundaries in a mobile app and move the herd remotely. Rotation, laneways, and fire breaks — without a single post in the ground.

Self-Powered

A triple energy-harvesting system — solar, body heat, and the motion of the ear itself — keeps each tag running continuously. No charging. No battery swaps.

Herd Intelligence

Temperature, location, and behavior stream from every animal over mesh, cellular, and satellite networks — turning health signals into early warnings.

The Hardware

Small on the ear. Big in the field.

A lightweight, weatherproof polymer twin-tag system: an identification disk facing forward, a computing housing behind the ear.
