Privacy and cookie choice

This page says exactly what headwayfence.com counts, what it stores in your browser, and how to change your mind.

What this site counts

The site counts page views: a number for each page and a number for each day. The counter sets no cookies and keeps no names, email addresses, payment details or location data. There is no account and no login, so nothing about you is asked for.

The counts are shown as totals only. A total cannot tell anyone who you are, and nothing here follows you around the web or shows you advertising. This site runs no ads.

What the Accept button adds

If you press Accept in the banner, one small flag is saved in this browser, on this device only. It records that you accepted and the date of a first visit that day, so repeat visitors are counted once per day. The flag has no name or account number attached. It is not a cookie and it is not sent to any advertiser.

How to opt out

Press Essential only in the banner and no flag is saved. Page views are still counted, because that total holds no personal data. If you pressed Accept and changed your mind, clear this site's data in your browser settings and the banner will ask again.

What is never collected

No name, address, phone number, password or card number. Email only reaches us when you choose to send it. No advertising trackers and no social media widgets.
