Privacy and cookie choice

What headwayfence.com counts, what it stores in your browser, and how to change your mind.

What this site counts

Page views only: a number for each page and each day. No cookies, no names, no email addresses, no location. There is no account and no login.

Nothing here follows you around the web. This site runs no ads.

What the Accept button adds

Press Accept and one small flag is saved in this browser, so a repeat visit is counted once a day. It has no name attached. It is not a cookie and it is not sent to any advertiser.

How to opt out

Press Essential only and no flag is saved. If you pressed Accept and changed your mind, clear this site's data in your browser and the banner will ask again.

What is never collected

No name, address, phone number, password or card number. Email only reaches us when you choose to send it.
