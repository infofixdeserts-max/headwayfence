How this site measures

Privacy and cookie choice

This page says exactly what headwayfence.com counts, what it stores in your browser, and how to change your mind. Nothing on this site is hidden behind small print.

What this site counts

The site counts page views. That means a number for each page, and a number for each day. The counter sets no cookies and keeps no names, no email addresses, no payment details, and no location data. There is no account on this site and there is no login, so nothing about you is asked for in the first place.

The counts are shown as totals only. A total cannot tell anyone who you are, and nothing here is used to follow you around the web or to show you advertising. This site runs no ads.

What the Accept button adds

If you press Accept in the banner at the bottom of the page, one small flag is saved in this browser, on this device only. The flag records that you accepted and the date of a first visit that day. It is used to count repeat visitors once per day instead of counting the same device on every page.

The flag has no name attached to it and no account number. It is not a cookie, and it is not sent to any advertiser. Deleting it changes nothing about how the site works for you.

How to opt out

Press Essential only in the banner and no flag is saved. Page views are still counted, because that total holds no personal data, but repeat visits are not.

If you pressed Accept earlier and changed your mind, clear this site's data in your browser settings. The banner will ask you again on your next visit. A private or incognito window also starts fresh every time you close it.

What is never collected

No name, no address, no phone number, no password, and no card number. Email only reaches this site when you choose to send it yourself. The pages carry no advertising trackers and no social media widgets that watch you while you read.

No cookies. No personal profiles. One optional flag on your own device, and you can delete it any time.
