Words we use

Plain meanings for the words on this site and in the Headway app.

Virtual fence
A fence drawn on a map in an app and held by a tag on each animal, with sound first instead of wire and posts.
Strip
A smaller part of a paddock that a mob grazes for a short time before moving on.
Strip grazing
Giving the herd a fresh strip of grass every day or two, with a back fence so grazed ground can rest.
Mob
A group of animals managed together, such as cows and calves or a group of steers.
Paddock
A fenced or virtually fenced area of pasture.
Keep-out zone
An area inside a paddock that animals are kept out of, such as a creek bank or the yard.
Rest period
Days since a paddock was last grazed. Good rest lets grass grow back.
Stocking rate
How many animals the land carries.
Grazing days
How many days a strip or paddock can feed a mob.
Holistic Management
A way of planning that looks at people, money and land together.
Walk-over scale
A scale in a lane, often on the way to water, that weighs each animal as it walks across.
Daily gain
How much weight an animal puts on each day.
Withdrawal period
Days after a treatment before an animal can be sold for meat or its milk used.
Tone
The sound a Headway tag plays as an animal walks toward the fence line. Most animals turn back on the tone.
Stimulus
The last step at the fence line, used only after the tones, with strict limits.
