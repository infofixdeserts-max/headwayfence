How to choose a virtual fence

Virtual fencing is a long-term choice for your operation. Here are the questions to ask any company before you buy, and how Headway answers them.

What does it cost per head per year, including data?

Headway's app is free. Tag pricing will be published before the first sale.

How long does it run through a cloudy week?

Headway tags check their position less often in a long cloudy spell, but never near the line, so the fence holds.

Does it work with no cell signal?

The grazing plan is stored on every Headway tag, so planned moves happen with no signal.

Can I keep animals out of a creek or the yard inside a paddock?

Yes. Headway has keep-out zones inside each paddock.

Can I pause it while I feed?

Yes. Pause a mob's tags from the app. They switch back on by themselves.

What happens to an animal that ends up outside the line?

The Headway tag goes quiet and tells you where the animal is, so it is never pushed away from home.

Is it worn on the neck or the ear?

Headway is an ear tag. There is no neck strap to stretch, snap or catch.

Is the app made for a computer, or only a phone?

The Headway app is made for a computer and tablet first, and works on a phone.

Does it help with the rest of the ranch?

The Headway app includes grazing plans, money and farm tax forms at no charge.

Can I talk to ranchers who use it?

Ask every company this. Headway's pilot ranches will be glad to talk once the pilot is running.

Do your homework

  • Ask each company to put you in touch with ranchers who use it.
  • Check prices in your own currency and ask what changes at renewal.
  • Ask what you need to buy besides the devices: base stations, towers or data plans.
  • Look at a real month of alerts from a ranch like yours.

Want Headway on your place?

Pilot tags go to Florida ranches first. Tell us about your herd and your ground.
