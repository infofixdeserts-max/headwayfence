How to choose a virtual fence

Questions to ask any company before you buy, and how Headway answers them.

What does it cost each year?

The app has no subscription. The tags run on a cost-effective subscription.

Does it keep working on cloudy days?

Yes. The tags are charged by the sun and the fence holds.

Does it work with no cell signal?

Yes. Each tag keeps the fence and the grazing plan on its own.

Can I keep stock off a creek or the yard?

Yes, with as many keep-out zones as you need.

Can I turn it off while I feed?

Yes. Pause a mob from the app, and turn it back on when you are done.

What if an animal gets out?

The tag goes quiet and shows you where it is.

Is it worn on the neck or the ear?

The ear. No collar to snap or catch.

Is the app only for phones?

No. It is made for computer and tablet, and works on a phone.

Does it help with the rest of the place?

Yes. Grazing plans, herd records, money and tax are in the same app.

Before you buy

  • Ask to talk to people who use it.
  • Ask what changes when you renew.
  • Ask what else you have to buy, such as towers, base stations or data plans.
