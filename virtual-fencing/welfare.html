Animal welfare comes first

Sound does the work. A mild stimulus is only ever the last step, and the tag has hard limits it cannot break.

Sound first, always

Every crossing starts with a tone, then a stronger tone. Most cattle never go further than that.

Never on the way back

An animal walking back toward the paddock is never cued. Turning back is the goal.

Not while running

A scared or running animal gets left alone.

Hard limits

A small number of stimuli per crossing and per day, with a gap between each one.

Too far out, go quiet

If an animal ends up well outside the line the tag stops cueing and tells you, so it is never stuck on the wrong side.

Unsure of position, no cue

If the tag is not sure where it is, it does nothing.

These rules are written into the tag software itself and tested every time it is built. They are not settings that can be switched off.
