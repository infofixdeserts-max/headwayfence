Breaks and strip grazing

Give the herd fresh grass every day or two without building a single temporary fence.

Templates

Pick 4 even strips, 3 strips with a back fence, or wedges that all touch water. The breaks are drawn for you.

Break numbers

Each break shows its area, the feed you can use, what the mob eats per day and how many grazing days it holds.

Move from the app

Tap Move a mob, pick the next break, done. The new line and a new grass chime go to every tag.

Back fence

Grazed ground behind the herd stays shut so it can rest and regrow.

Several mobs

Run cows and calves and a mob of steers on the same place, each with its own line.

Undo

Moved the wrong mob? Undo is right there for a few seconds.
