Strips and strip grazing

Give the herd fresh grass every day or two without building a single temporary fence.

Ready-made strips

Pick a layout and the strips are drawn for you.

Days of grass

Each strip shows how many days of grazing it holds.

Move in one tap

Pick the next strip. The herd hears a new grass chime and moves on.

Back fence

Grazed ground stays shut so it can regrow.

Several mobs

Each mob gets its own fence.

Undo

Moved the wrong mob? Undo it.
