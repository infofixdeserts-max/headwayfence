How a virtual fence works

Draw the line on a map. The tags hold it. Research on virtual fencing shows most cattle learn the line within a few days, mostly from sound.

1

Draw it

Trace a paddock, a strip or a keep-out zone on the farm map. Save it and it goes to every tag in the mob.

2

Warn it

As a cow walks toward the line her tag plays a tone. Most cattle turn around right there.

3

Hold it

Keep walking and the tone gets stronger. A mild stimulus is the last step, with hard limits. Too far out and the tag goes quiet and tells you.

Cross fence a pasture with a few clicks.

Grazing breaks, creek banks, under-used corners, water points and pivot towers can all be fenced in or fenced out from the map. Choosing where cattle graze and for how long is how you build soil health and better grass.

A virtual fence is a management tool. It is not a legal perimeter fence, and it does not replace the permanent fence along your road and boundary.
