Guide, split and move the herd

The virtual line can move. That means you can walk a mob to new ground, hold it in a lane, or split it, all from the app.

Guide a mob

Pick a mob and where it should go. The app draws a lane from where they are to the new paddock, and the line walks them along it a little at a time. You watch them go on the map.

Split a mob

Pull the calves off the cows, the heavy steers off the light ones, or the cows due to calve into their own mob. Each new mob gets its own line on every tag.

Move on a schedule

Put moves in the grazing plan and they happen on time, every time, with or without signal.

Back into the pens

Bring a mob up the lane to the yard for working, then pause their tags while they are in.
