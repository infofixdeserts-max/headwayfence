Automatic weighing

Know what every animal weighs without running the herd through the chute. Weights come in on a schedule you pick.

How it works

  • Set up a walk-over scale in the lane to water.
  • Pick how often: every 3, 7, 14 or 30 days.
  • On weigh days the herd is guided to water through the scale lane.
  • Each animal is weighed as it walks over, and the weight lands on its record.

Every Headway tag carries a standard livestock EID chip, so scales and readers that read EID tags already know who is on the scale.

Daily gain

Weight gain per day for each animal, each mob and the whole herd.

Who is falling behind

An animal that stops gaining goes to the top of your list. That is often the first sign of trouble.

Sell on time

See which steers are at target weight this week, without guessing.
