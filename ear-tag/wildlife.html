Land and wildlife

Your herd walks every corner of your place. Their tags see and hear it, so you know what is living on your land.

Grass and plants

Camera tags take pictures of the ground as the herd grazes. See grass cover, bare ground and which plants are coming in.

Weeds

Spot a weed patch early, and drop it on today's job list.

Birds

Every tag listens. Bird song tells you which birds live on your land, and how that changes year to year.

Insects

Hear the buzz of bees, crickets and other insects. More life in the grass is a sign of healthy land.

Predators

The tags listen for wolves, coyotes and dogs, and notice when the herd bunches up or runs. You get an alert and where it happened.

Other wildlife

Deer, hogs and other animals the camera tags catch, marked on your map.

See your land get better

Good grazing brings more grass, more plants, more birds and more insects. Headway keeps a record of all of it, season by season, paddock by paddock.

  • A map of what lives where.
  • A yearly report for your land.
  • Proof of the good you are doing, for buyers, programs and the next generation.
