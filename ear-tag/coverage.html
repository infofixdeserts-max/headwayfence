Places with no cell signal

Ranches have dead spots. Headway is built to keep working in them.

Plan stored on the tag

The grazing plan lives on every tag, so moves happen on time even when nothing can get through.

Cell first

Tags send their news over the low-power cellular networks made for devices like this.

Low-data app mode

The app sends only what it must, so it works over phone satellite messaging where your carrier and phone support it.

Herd link, in development

Tags passing messages tag to tag, so a cow down in a draw is heard through her herd mates up on the ridge.

Yard base station, in development

Where there is no tower at all, a base station in the yard carries the link.

Satellite tags, planned

A satellite link for the tags themselves, for open range with no towers.
