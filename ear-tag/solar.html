Home / The ear tag / Solar power

Charged by the sun, right on the ear

A small solar panel on every tag tops it up each day. The tag is sealed for life. No batteries to swap, no charging station.

Sealed for life outside

Rain, mud and dust stay out.

Looks after its own battery

In a cloudy week the tag checks its position less often, but never near the line, so the fence holds.

Built for updates

The tag is built to take new features over the air, so nobody has to catch a cow.

Pilot tags go to Florida ranches first. Tell us about your herd and your ground.
