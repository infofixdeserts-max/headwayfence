Tap a tag for the record

Hold a phone to the ear tag and that animal's record opens. Made for the vet in the pen and the rancher in the paddock.

  • Phone tap: the tag can be read by a phone held close to it.
  • No signal needed for the basics: the tag carries its ID and key health notes, such as the last treatment and the withdrawal date.
  • Standard EID too: chute readers and scales that read livestock EID tags can read it.
  • Add a treatment right there, and it is saved to the record.
