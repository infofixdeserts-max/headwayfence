Health monitoring

The herd tag watches every animal, day and night. You hear about it when something changes.

Heart rate

A heart rate that climbs or drops from that animal's normal is often the first sign of trouble.

Temperature

Spot a fever early, before the animal goes off its feed.

Blood oxygen

Another early sign when an animal is not right.

Movement

Grazing, resting and walking each day. Less grazing or more lying down shows up fast.

Heat stress

The tag feels the heat and humidity around the herd, and warns you before it gets dangerous.

Your own alerts

Set alerts for what matters to you, like a cow going off alone to calve.

Catch it early

Each animal has its own normal. Headway learns it, and tells you when that animal drifts from it. You check the one that needs you, not the whole herd.

Every reading goes on the animal's record, ready for your vet.
