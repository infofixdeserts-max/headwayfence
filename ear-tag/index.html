What the Headway ear tag keeps track of

Every animal, all day and all night, and you hear about it only when it matters.

An ear tag, not a collar.

Ranchers already put tags in ears. There is no neck strap to stretch, snap or catch on a fence, and no collar to fit on a hairy breed.

Each animal wears a pair: one tag on each side of the ear, working together.

Where every animal is

GPS position on the farm map, live.

How it spends the day

Grazing, resting and walking, from how it moves.

Who left the herd

Standing apart for hours often means calving, sick or hurt.

Not moving

An animal that has not moved since morning goes to the top of your list.

Theft

Moving at truck speed sends an alert right away.

Water

How often the herd visits each trough, so a stuck float shows the same day.

Lightning

How far away and whether it is getting closer.

Find a tag

Play a locate tone from the app to find an animal in the brush.

Out of contact

If a tag has not checked in for a day you are told, and it keeps holding its line on its own.
