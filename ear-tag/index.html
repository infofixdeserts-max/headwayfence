The Headway ear tag

Know where every animal is and how it is doing. You only hear about it when it matters.

An ear tag, not a collar.

It goes in the ear like the tags you already use. No neck strap to stretch, snap or catch on a fence.

Three tags, one herd

Herd tag

Goes on every animal. Holds the fence and watches heart rate, temperature and movement.

Lead tag

Goes in the other ear of a few animals. Finds the herd on the map, sends news to your app and hears lightning.

Camera tag

Goes in the other ear of a few animals. Takes pictures of the grass, plants and wildlife where the herd grazes.

Where they are

Every animal on your map.

How they are doing

Heart rate, temperature and blood oxygen, plus grazing, resting and walking.

Heat stress

Know when the herd is getting too hot.

Off on its own

An animal standing apart often means calving, sick or hurt.

Not moving

Goes straight to the top of your list.

Leaving the place

If animals move fast off your place, you know right away.

Lightning

Know when a storm is close.

Your own alerts

Set your own, like a cow going off alone to calve.

Lost contact

If a tag goes quiet for a day, you are told. It keeps the fence on its own.
